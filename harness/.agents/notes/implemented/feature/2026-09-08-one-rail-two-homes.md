# Agent Note: One rail, two homes — the collapsed sidebar shows the minimode bar's rail, and the floating bar renders the same component

Status: implemented

JJ, 8 Sep 2026, review item 26: "collapse sidebar vs minimode — aligned/combined". The choice: "One rail component, two homes".

## Problem

The collapsed sidebar and the floating minimode bar showed different things. The collapsed 56px rail (`@deepseek-ai/dsh-client-ui-sidebar`'s `SidebarRoot`) rendered ui-workspace's icon column of projects and spaces through `sidebar.workspaces`; the Askbar window (`@idealize/askbar`'s `AskbarRoot`) rendered the project cell, the Studio entry with the owl, one chip per agent of the current project with its state, the hold-to-talk flow and the compact panel. The two are the same idea — the sidebar at its narrowest — and a person who learned one found the other showed unrelated rows. `AskbarRoot` was one component that owned the drag region, the transform route and the whole body, so the body could not render anywhere but the Askbar window; the sidebar shell had no seat for a collapsed-only occupant, and the Studio open lived behind a host-bridge feed event the floating bar reached through the transform route, which the main window has no reason to call.

## Decision

**The bar body is a `Rail` component with two homes.** `Rail` (`packages/idealize/askbar/src/client/Rail.tsx`) takes the bar view (project, roster, error), a `home` (`floating` or `sidebar`), the locale seat, `onOpenMain(open?: 'studio')` for what the project cell (no argument) and the Studio entry plus the panel foot (`'studio'`) do, and an optional `onOpenChip(chip)` that replaces the panel on a chip click. The hold flow, the pending-send countdown, the `Panel` and the keyboard path stay in the rail, so a hold reaches the panel in both homes; the panel's ask field posts to the agent through comm, which works from any window. The rail element carries `data-askbar-rail` and `data-home` in both homes. `AskbarRoot` is the floating home: it owns the store subscription, the `data-askbar-bar` drag-region root docked to the configured edge, and `transform()`, and renders `<Rail home='floating'>`. `SidebarRail` is the sidebar home: it renders `<Rail home='sidebar'>` with `onOpenMain` expanding the sidebar or opening the Studio, and `onOpenChip` opening the chip's chat through `ctx.sessions.open`. In the sidebar home the rail forces its panel edge to `left`, since the sidebar sits at the window's left edge whatever edge the floating bar docks to.

**A `sidebar.rail` seat in the sidebar shell, swapped in by occupancy.** `SidebarRoot` declares `sidebar.rail` (single, root, owner `{ expandSidebar }`) beside its other seats. The plugin injects a `useRailOccupied` hook built on `ctx.slots.subscribe('sidebar.rail')` and `ctx.slots.entriesOfSlot('sidebar.rail').length > 0`; while the column is collapsed (past the crossfade settle) and the seat is occupied, the browsing region renders `sidebar.rail`, otherwise it renders `sidebar.workspaces` at the position it always had. Occupancy is read from the registry rather than through `renderSlot`'s `fallback` option because the fallback would nest the browser outlet inside the rail outlet, and the outlet is one unkeyed React component type: the browser would remount at every collapse and expand even in a composition with no rail registrant, losing its typed search. With the registry read, a composition without `@idealize/askbar` renders exactly what it rendered before, and the shell's snapshots did not change.

**The askbar plugin registers both homes from one `apply`.** The dictionaries register in every window. With the `dsh-desktop-mode=askbar` marker the plugin marks the page and registers `AskbarRoot` into `root` at priority -1 as before. Without it, `ctx.slots.inject('sidebar.rail', …)` seats `SidebarRail` once the sidebar declares the seat; the store starts inside that injection, so a composition without a sidebar never polls the roster. Both homes share `follow()`: `startAskbarStore` on the current selection, re-selected on every session or workspace store change.

**The in-window Studio open reuses the one Studio rule.** `@idealize/ui-bar`'s `openStudio` was reachable only through the host-bridge feed's `open-studio` event, which the transform route pushes for the floating bar. The sidebar rail raises `requestStudio()` (`packages/idealize/askbar/src/client/studio-request.ts`), a `CustomEvent` named `idealize:open-studio` on `document`; ui-bar's existing effect adds `onStudioRequest(() => openStudio())` beside its feed listener and disposes both together. The module is one constant and two DOM calls, inlined into each bundle the way `@idealize/artefacts`'s `reveal.ts` already is, so neither package value-imports the other's bundle and no second Studio-opening rule exists.

**Layout for 36px.** The collapsed column's content width is 36px (56px minus the shell's 10px insets). `.rail[data-home='sidebar']` sets the project cell, Studio entry and chips to 36px, the brand mark to 36px (`BrandMark size={36}`), avatars to 32px, 12px between chips (the collapsed column's rhythm), and hides the captions, state labels and the empty and error lines, which a 36px column cannot hold. The floating `.bar` keeps its 72px width, 58px mark, 20px chip gap, background tokens and drag region. Every colour stays a theme token; `askbar-css.spec.ts` gained a check on the compact rules.

**Copy.** `project.expandSidebar` ("{project} — expand the sidebar") and `studio.openHere` ("Studio — open it") in English and Simplified Chinese, used only by the sidebar home; the floating home keeps `project.expand` and `studio.open`.

## Alternatives considered

**Render the rail hidden while wide and let the occupant decide.** The seat would then depend on every occupant rendering nothing while wide, and the rail's store subscription would run inside a hidden component. The shell owns the decision and never renders the seat while wide.

**`renderSlot('sidebar.rail', …, { fallback: renderSlot('sidebar.workspaces', …) })`.** The documented empty-slot fallback, but it nests the browser outlet inside the rail outlet only when collapsed, so the browser remounts at each collapse and expand in every composition, rail or not, and the shell's collapsed snapshot changes. The registry read keeps upstream behaviour byte-for-byte without a registrant.

**Post the transform route with `open: 'studio'` from the main window.** The route pushes the feed event the main window already answers, but it also asks the desktop shell to expand a window that is already expanded, and it answers 501 without a shell. The document event reaches the same `openStudio` with no host round trip and no window transform.

**A shared package for the Studio request.** A workspace package for one constant and two DOM calls; the fork already inlines such a module across bundles for the galleries' Reveal signal, so the same pattern applies.

**Start the sidebar home's store only while the column is collapsed.** It would spare the main window a roster poll and a hurry-up stream while the rail is not showing, at the cost of a first-collapse empty flash while the first read lands. Deferred; noted in the README's limitations.

## Consequences

In the main window the collapsed sidebar shows the project's agents with their states, the Studio entry and the project mark; a chip click opens that chat, a hold opens the panel, the Studio entry opens the Studio chat in place, and the project cell expands the sidebar. The project browser's rail icons (projects, spaces, add, search) no longer show while collapsed in the IDEalize composition; expanding the sidebar shows the browser. The browser unmounts at the collapse settle and remounts on expand with the rail seated, so a typed search does not survive a collapse in this composition. The main window polls the roster route and holds a `/idealize/events/stream` hurry-up from the moment the sidebar declares the seat. `@idealize/ui-bar` depends on `@idealize/askbar` (the inlined `studio-request` module), and `@idealize/askbar` peer-depends on `@deepseek-ai/dsh-client-ui-sidebar` for the `sidebar.rail` type merge. Upstream touch logged in FORK.md: `packages/client/ui-sidebar` gains the seat, the injected hook and the swap; `slot-catalog.ts` regenerated with `sidebar.rail`.

Walk checks (`landing-walk-proof.mjs` section s) keep their meaning for the floating home: `[data-askbar-bar]` is still the drag-region root and paints the sidebar background; its first `img` is the 58px brand mark; the Studio button is still the second button and contains the owl; chips still live under `[role=list]` with `data-chip-id`; every button still reads `no-drag`. The rail element inside it is new (`[data-askbar-rail][data-home="floating"]`), and the arrow-key handler moved from the bar to the rail element. A main-window check for the sidebar home would query `[data-askbar-rail][data-home="sidebar"]` inside `[data-slot="sidebar.rail"]` after collapsing the sidebar.

Tests: `askbar/tests/rail.client.spec.tsx` (both homes' callbacks, the compact layout, `SidebarRail` wiring), `askbar/tests/askbar-root.client.spec.tsx` (the drag-region root around the rail), `askbar/tests/apply.client.spec.tsx` (the marker branch and the `sidebar.rail` branch, `openSession` through the sessions store, `openStudio` raising the request), `askbar/tests/askbar-css.spec.ts`, `ui-sidebar/tests/sidebar-root.client.spec.tsx` and `rail-seat.client.spec.tsx` (renders the registrant collapsed, the browser wide or with the seat empty, swaps live as the seat fills and empties), `ui-sidebar/tests/apply.client.spec.tsx`, `ui-bar/tests/studio-card.client.spec.tsx` (the in-window request opens the Studio and stops on dispose).
